Acoustic monitoring of environmental noise

Audio ML · Classification

Product
Noisix, Amstein + Walthert Group
Role
Lead; developed nearly everything from scratch
Shown at
Bauphysikertreffen, HFT Stuttgart, 2025

Noisix is an ML system that classifies the sources of environmental noise, built for the acoustics practice of Amstein + Walthert Group. Instead of only measuring how loud a site is, the monitoring tells you what is making the noise.

I led the project and developed nearly all of it from scratch. It positioned the group as a leader in this niche, and I presented it at the Bauphysikertreffen at HFT Stuttgart in 2025.
