Artificial coupling of patterned neural networks on HD-MEAs

HD-MEA · Embedded · Electrophysiology

Type
Bachelor's thesis
Lab
Laboratory of Biosensors and Bioelectronics, ETH Zurich
Year
2024

The goal was to couple two feed-forward neuronal networks, grown in patterns on a high-density microelectrode array, into one recurrent network using closed-loop stimulation.

I built the full pipeline: embedded closed-loop stimulation, experiment control and data analysis.

Result

Higher stimulus amplitudes reliably increased the share of stimuli that triggered oscillations.
