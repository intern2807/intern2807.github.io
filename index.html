About me

I'm a Senior Data Scientist at one of Switzerland's largest financial institutions, modelling transaction data. Before that, I spent five years at a Swiss engineering group, from working student in acoustics to interim Head of AI. I built ML-based acoustic monitoring of environmental noise (presented at the Bauphysikertreffen, HFT Stuttgart), energy monitoring for 45,000+ smart meters, and new group-level data services.

I'm completing my MSc in Electrical Engineering at ETH Zurich, focused on ML and neuroscience. In 2025, I worked as a project researcher at the Translational Neuromodeling Unit (University of Zurich & ETH Zurich) and LMU Munich, developing AI models for neuroscience. For my bachelor's thesis at ETH's Laboratory of Biosensors and Bioelectronics (2024), I built an end-to-end experimental pipeline for neuronal networks on high-density microelectrode arrays.

Before moving into AI in 2021, I studied in the BFA Performer-Composer program at CalArts and built a music career: 2.5M+ streams, a win at the UK Songwriting Contest, a John Lennon Songwriting Contest finalist spot, TV and radio play, and a feature in WIRED. I also co-founded a music studio, which I later sold.
